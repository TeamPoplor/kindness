How it works

Think of it as kindness bingo.

1

Fill your board

Add a person and an act of kindness to each square. Just a name or an act is fine to start! Cross off each square when you finish, and try to complete at least a line (row, column, or diagonal) by the end of November, or better yet, every square.

2

Let us know!

No matter how far you get, report your acts of kindness once you're done, so we can see the difference we've made together.

3

Get others involved

Magnify your impact by inviting others to join. Friends and family can download their own board right here.

Get your board

Didn't get a printed board? No problem. Download one, print it at home, and you're ready to go. Print a few extra to share!

Letter (U.S.) A4 (international)

Two pages: the board and the instructions. Print double-sided if you can.

Report your kindness

Every act counts, whether you finished one square or all nine. When you're done, tell us how far you got and we'll add you to the count.

Why we started

The Pilgrimage of Kindness started as a simple idea: the world would be a better place if people were nicer to each other.

We thought about how to bring the idea to life, and The Pilgrimage of Kindness was born. Think of it as kindness bingo :)

We hope you will join us by completing a board yourself, and that you will share the pilgrimage with others!

Thank you for your support!
